Contact Us

Have a question about the calculator, spotted an error in one of our guides, or want to suggest a feature? We read every message and use your feedback to make RecompForge better. Reach out and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

Get in Touch

The best way to reach us is by email. Whether you have a quick question or detailed feedback, we are happy to hear from you. Send your message to:

Email:RecompForge@outlook.com

We are a small independent team, so we do not have a live chat or instant support line. Every message goes to a real person who will read it and respond thoughtfully. We aim to reply to all inquiries within 2 to 3 business days.

What to Include

To help us give you the most useful response, please include as much relevant detail as possible in your message.

For Calculator Issues

Let us know the inputs you used — age, gender, weight, height, activity level, and goal — along with a description of the result you received and what you expected to see. If you noticed an error message or unexpected behaviour, describing exactly what happened helps us reproduce and fix the issue quickly.

For Content Corrections

If you have found a factual inaccuracy in one of our guides or articles, please include the page title or URL, the specific claim you believe is incorrect, and a source or reference that supports the correction. We take accuracy seriously and will review every correction request promptly.

For Feature Requests

Tell us what you would like the calculator or site to do that it does not currently do, and why you think it would be useful. We cannot promise to build every suggestion, but we read all of them and they directly inform what we work on next.

Response Time

We aim to respond to all messages within 2 to 3 business days. During busy periods it may occasionally take a little longer, but we will always get back to you. If you have not received a reply after 5 business days, please check your spam folder and feel free to send a follow-up.

Before You Write

If your question is about how the calculator works — which formulas it uses, what the numbers mean, or how to interpret your results — you may find a faster answer in our guides section. We have written detailed explanations of every aspect of the calculation process.

Common Topics

These are the most common reasons people get in touch with us. If yours fits one of these categories, the detail above should help you write a message we can act on quickly.

Calculator Feedback

Suggestions for improving the calculator interface, additional inputs you would like to see, or feedback on the clarity of the results page.

Bug Reports

Anything that appears broken, produces an unexpected result, or does not display correctly on your device or browser.

Content Questions

Questions about the science behind our guides, requests for clarification on a specific recommendation, or suggestions for topics you would like us to cover.

General Feedback

Anything else — we welcome all of it. If something about RecompForge helped you or frustrated you, we want to know.

Privacy Inquiries

If your message relates to your personal data — such as a request to access, correct, or delete information we hold about you — please send it to the same address and include as much detail as possible about your request. We handle all privacy-related inquiries in accordance with our Privacy Policy and will respond within 30 days as required.

Email:RecompForge@outlook.com

We typically respond to all inquiries within 2 to 3 business days.
