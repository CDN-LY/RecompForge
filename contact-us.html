Contact Us

Got a question, found a mistake, or want to suggest something? We read every message. Send us an email and we'll get back to you.

Get in Touch

Email us at: RecompForge@outlook.com

We're a small independent team — no live chat, just real people reading your message. We reply to everything within 2–3 business days.

What to Include

A little detail helps us reply faster. Here's what to mention depending on your topic:

Calculator Issue

Share the inputs you used — age, gender, weight, height, activity level, and goal — and describe what result you got vs. what you expected.

Content Correction

Tell us the page or article, what you think is wrong, and include a source if you have one. We take accuracy seriously and will look into it quickly.

Feature Request

Tell us what you'd like the site or calculator to do and why it would help. We can't build everything, but we read all suggestions and they shape what we work on next.

Response Time

We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. If it's been 5 days and you haven't heard back, check your spam folder and feel free to follow up.

Privacy Inquiries

Want to access, correct, or delete your data? Send your request to the same email address with as much detail as possible. We follow our Privacy Policy and respond within 30 days.

Email: RecompForge@outlook.com
