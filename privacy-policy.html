PrivacyPolicy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information RecompForge collects when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices available to you. We are committed to handling your data transparently and responsibly.

Overview

RecompForge operates the website RecompForge.com, a free body recomposition calculator that helps you estimate daily calorie targets, macronutrient breakdowns, and fitness goals. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the site.

RecompForge does not sell your personal data. The calculator runs entirely in your browser — your health inputs such as weight, height, and age are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Information We Collect

We collect two types of information: information you provide directly, and information collected automatically when you visit our site.

Information You Provide

Calculator inputs including age, gender, weight, height, activity level, and fitness goal are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to our servers. If you contact us via email, we receive your name, email address, and the content of your message.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the site, we automatically receive certain technical data including your IP address, browser type and version, the pages you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the referring URL. We also collect device data such as device type, operating system, and screen resolution. This data is used solely for site operation and aggregated analytics.

A Note on Health Data

We never collect sensitive health data such as medical records, prescriptions, or clinical diagnoses. The inputs you enter into the calculator are used for on-device computation only and are not associated with your identity in any way.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve RecompForge. This includes delivering page content and ensuring technical functionality, understanding how visitors use the site so we can improve the calculator and content, responding to inquiries or support requests you send us, meeting our obligations under applicable laws, and detecting and preventing fraudulent or malicious activity.

We do not use your data for targeted advertising, profiling, or any automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and gather analytics data. Cookies are small data files stored on your device by your browser.

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These are required for the website to function properly and cannot be disabled. They do not collect any personally identifiable information.

Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how visitors interact with the site through tools such as Google Analytics. All data collected through analytics is aggregated and anonymised. You may opt out at any time via your browser settings or the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on available from Google.

Preference Cookies

These remember your unit preferences such as kilograms versus pounds so you do not need to reselect them on each visit. Disabling these cookies will not affect the calculator's core functionality.

Third-Party Services

We use a small number of reputable third-party services to operate and improve the site. Google Analytics collects anonymised usage data and is governed byGoogle's Privacy Policy. Bootstrap stylesheets and icon fonts are delivered via the jsDelivr CDN, which may log access requests; see thejsDelivr Privacy Policyfor details. Our web hosting provider processes server logs including IP addresses for security and uptime monitoring purposes.

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties beyond those listed above, unless required by law.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect the limited data we handle from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

All traffic between your browser and our website is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS). Calculator computations happen entirely in your browser, so your health inputs never leave your device. Server logs are retained for no more than 90 days, after which they are permanently deleted.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. If you are located in the EU or EEA, the General Data Protection Regulation grants you the right to access, correct, delete, and port your personal data. If you are in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act provides similar rights including the right to know what data we collect and the right to opt out of its sale — though we do not sell personal data.

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data, to request deletion of your personal data where applicable, and to opt out of analytics tracking at any time. You may also request your data in a machine-readable format where technically feasible. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in the final section. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

RecompForge is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. The calculator is intended for adults or teenagers using it under parental supervision.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete such information from our systems.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the last updated date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify users, such as posting a notice on the homepage.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to exercise your data rights, or need to get in touch for any other reason, please reach out to us directly.

Email:RecompForge@outlook.com

We typically respond to all inquiries within 2 to 3 business days.
