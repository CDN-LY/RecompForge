About RecompForge

RecompForge is a free, science-backed body recomposition calculator built for people who want to lose fat and build muscle at the same time — without wading through misinformation, supplement ads, or overcomplicated advice. We built this tool because we wished it had existed when we started our own fitness journeys.

Who We Are

RecompForge was created by a small team of fitness enthusiasts, nutrition hobbyists, and developers who share a frustration with the way fitness information is typically presented online. Too much of it is driven by product sales, influencer incentives, or outdated broscience. We wanted to build something different — a straightforward, honest tool grounded in peer-reviewed research.

We are not a gym, a supplement company, a coaching service, or a media brand. We do not sell products or take sponsorships from brands that might influence our recommendations. RecompForge is an independent project built and maintained by people who care about getting this right.

We are not doctors, registered dietitians, or certified personal trainers. RecompForge is an educational resource, not a clinical service. We always encourage users to consult qualified professionals before making significant changes to their diet or exercise routine.

Why We Built RecompForge

The body recomposition space is full of noise. A quick search for calorie calculators returns dozens of tools that produce wildly different numbers with no explanation of the underlying formulas. Fitness forums debate protein requirements using anecdotes. Influencers promote extreme deficits or unnecessary supplements to audiences who trust them.

At the same time, the actual science of body recomposition — how to calculate TDEE accurately, how much protein is genuinely needed, what deficit is optimal for fat loss without muscle loss — is well-established and accessible. It just is not packaged in a way that most people can easily use.

RecompForge exists to close that gap. We took the validated research on energy expenditure, protein requirements, and body composition and built a clean, transparent calculator around it. Every number the calculator produces can be traced back to a specific formula or published study. There is no proprietary algorithm, no hidden multiplier, and no upsell.

Our Approach

Everything at RecompForge starts with the research. When we write a guide or set a default value in the calculator, we reference peer-reviewed literature — not popular fitness books, YouTube channels, or supplement company white papers. We update our approach when new evidence emerges and we acknowledge openly when the science is uncertain or contested.

Transparency Over Simplicity

We prefer to explain our reasoning rather than just hand you a number. When the calculator gives you a calorie target, we tell you which formula was used and why. When a guide recommends a protein intake, we cite the studies behind the recommendation. We believe you are capable of understanding the rationale, and that understanding it makes you more likely to apply it consistently.

Honesty About Uncertainty

Population-average formulas are estimates, not measurements. Individual metabolism, genetics, hormonal profile, and lifestyle factors all influence outcomes in ways no calculator can fully capture. We are upfront about this. Our goal is to give you the best possible starting point, not to pretend that a formula can perfectly predict your body's behaviour.

The Calculator

The RecompForge calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — the most extensively validated BMR formula for general adult populations — to estimate your basal metabolic rate. This is then multiplied by an activity factor to produce your Total Daily Energy Expenditure. From there, the calculator applies your selected goal to recommend a daily calorie target and derives protein, carbohydrate, and fat targets based on your lean body mass.

The entire calculation happens locally in your browser. Your inputs are never sent to a server, stored in a database, or linked to your identity. You can use the calculator as many times as you like with complete privacy.

Why the Calculator Is Free

We believe access to accurate nutrition information should not be paywalled. RecompForge is and will remain free to use. The site is supported by non-intrusive display advertising that does not influence our recommendations in any way.

Our Content

Beyond the calculator, RecompForge publishes in-depth guides on the science of body recomposition, nutrition, and resistance training. Our articles are written to be genuinely useful to someone who wants to understand the topic, not just to rank in search engines.

We cover topics including how to set and adjust calorie targets over time, the role of protein in muscle retention during a deficit, how to structure a resistance training programme for recomposition, realistic timelines and expectations, and common mistakes that slow progress. Each guide is written with the same commitment to accuracy and transparency that underpins the calculator.

Our Values

Science First

Every recommendation we make is grounded in peer-reviewed research. We do not promote trends, fad diets, or approaches that lack a meaningful evidence base. When the science is clear, we say so. When it is genuinely uncertain or contested among researchers, we say that too.

No Conflicts of Interest

We do not accept paid partnerships, sponsored content, or affiliate arrangements with supplement brands, meal delivery services, or coaching programmes. Our recommendations are independent of commercial influence.

Accessibility

Accurate fitness information should be available to everyone, not just people who can afford a personal trainer or dietitian. RecompForge is free, ad-light, and designed to be usable by anyone with a basic understanding of their own body and goals.

Honesty

We will not tell you what you want to hear if it is not accurate. Body recomposition takes months of consistent effort. The calculator cannot do the work for you. We would rather give you a realistic picture than promise dramatic results that set you up for disappointment.

Who RecompForge Is For

RecompForge is built for adults who want to improve their body composition — reducing body fat and building or preserving lean muscle — using a structured, evidence-based approach. It is particularly well-suited to people who are new to tracking nutrition and want a clear starting framework, people returning to fitness after a break who want to recalibrate their targets, and intermediate trainees who want to optimise their nutrition around their training without overcomplicating it.

It is not a replacement for personalised guidance from a registered dietitian or a qualified personal trainer, particularly for people with medical conditions, disordered eating histories, or highly specific athletic goals. For those situations, we strongly encourage working with a professional.
